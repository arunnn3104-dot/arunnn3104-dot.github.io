Research

Safe to speak, not to judge.

People speak more openly to an AI interviewer than to a person, and trust it to record what they say and spot patterns across many people. They don't trust it to judge them as individuals unless a named human stays accountable.

When the Interviewer Becomes Artificial: Perception and Experience of AI-Led Market Research Interviews, my summer internship report (September 2026). Sixteen adults in the Netherlands each took an AI-led conversation, then a 19-question reflection interview. Thematic analysis turned 31 codes into 8 themes and 4 findings.
